Two supporting documents containing the results reported in the article "Enumeration of Legendre pairs of length l = 51" by  D.Crnković, D. Dumičić Danilović and Danijel Šanko are available for free download here.
One document contains a list of all mutually nonequivalent Legendre pairs of length 51, and the other a list of mutually nonisomorphic 2-(51,25,24) designs that correspond to the pairs.
